# The development Mac stays under the conditions the receiver of the app is under

When: a permission, a signature or a path makes the development loop awkward, and the way out
is a convenience only this machine would have — a signing certificate of its own, a local
config file, a special path.
Do: say what keeping the two the same costs, and ask before building the convenience. Fix the
cause where the person handed the app meets it — a sentence in the interface, a section in the
documentation — and leave this Mac in their conditions. Measure the mechanism either way: the
decision rests on how the thing works, not on "it worked today".
Why: a nuisance worked around on our side stops being noticed, and then it never gets fixed
where it hurts.
