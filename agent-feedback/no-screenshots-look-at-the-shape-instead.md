# A visual criterion is checked by the user's eyes or by an offline render, never by a screenshot

When: a phase ends in "read it on the real menu bar" — a new sign, a colour, a layout that must
not shift its neighbours.
Do: do not reach for `screencapture`. It is denied in this environment and fails with
`could not create image from display`, whatever the rect. Get the evidence two ways instead.
Draw the figure into a PNG from a throwaway rig in the scratchpad — copy the geometry numbers
out of the source, render the whole badge at 8x for the proportions and at 2x for what the eye
actually gets, and read the file. Then have the user look at the running bar and say what they
see; for a state that only a live session produces, ask them with `AskUserQuestion` while the
state is up — the question itself is what puts the session into it.
Why: a rig proves the shape, only a person proves the bar, and two hours can go into trying to
photograph a menu bar that this environment will never hand over.
